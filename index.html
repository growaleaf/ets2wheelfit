What this counts

"Physical H-pattern positions" are the forward gear gates plus reverse, counted from the shifter manufacturer's own product page. "Gearbox mode" requirements are read directly from SCS Software's own ETS2/ATS input configuration reference page, which documents the game's gearbox as twelve forward gears and one reverse gear, reached through three shifter configurations: Range Transmission, Splitter Transmission, and a combined Range-Splitter Transmission that emulates a real Scania H-shifter. "Clutch axis" reports whether the wheel's own bundled pedal set includes a clutch pedal, read from that wheel's own product page.

This tool reports counts and manufacturer statements. It does not tell you a combo is required, sufficient, or the best choice for ETS2 or ATS — that judgment is yours.
